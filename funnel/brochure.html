About Easy Plans DB

Built properly.
Managed by a professional.

Easy Plans DB is a design and build contractor based in Woodford, Essex. The business was founded by a building surveyor and a builder, who have more than 30 years of combined experience in construction.

We handle every stage ourselves: initial drawings and design, planning permission, building regulations approval, project management and the build itself. That means you never have to manage a separate architect, structural engineer, surveyor and builder.

What sets us apart

Three things most builders can't say.

01

A surveyor runs your job

A qualified building surveyor oversees your project from the first site visit to handover. It's a professional credential, not a sales rep's promise.

02

Design and build in one

Drawings, planning and building regs are handled for you. You won't need to hire an architect and then go looking for a builder.

03

Our own trades

Our qualified bricklayers, carpenters, roofers, electricians, plumbers and plasterers work on your job. We don't subcontract it out.
